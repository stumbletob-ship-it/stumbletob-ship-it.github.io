Affenplatte

A bamboo platform in the middle of the water. You have no legs – only arms.

  • Walk: put your hands on the ground and push back
  • Jump: push down hard and let go
  • Turn: right stick left/right
  • Hold a vine: hold the grip button and swing your arm
  • Fingers: trigger = index finger, grip = other fingers, thumb on a button or stick = thumb down
  • Bananas: grab them and sell them in the basket at the start for Crumbs
  • Backpack: buy one at the backpack shop, grab it from your back or belly, put bananas in, drop them with B / Y
  • Item shop: rocket, turtle, stilts, flower (use up to 3 – each one more spot for a friend to hold on), vine gun, teleport core, raft package and balloon (half gravity)
  • Belt: let go of an item at your right hip to put it in your belt, grab there to take it out
  • Menu: click the left stick (Crumbs and backpack)
  • Talk: just speak – in a room the others hear you (louder when they are close)
  • Careful: the swamp is toxic – touching it sends you back to the start. In Hunt n Run there is thick brush instead – it slows you down

Cross the stones in the water to reach the bush – it takes you into the jungle.
